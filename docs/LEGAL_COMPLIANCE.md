## 1. FCA Opinion Letter (Required for UK)

**Proposed Classification:** Unregulated financial advisory tool

**Rationale:**
- No investment advice given (AI generates signals, not recommendations)
- Users make own trading decisions
- Disclaimers prevent promotion as financial advice
- Risk controls prevent excessive leverage

```

DATE: [LAUNCH DATE]

   - Users must complete KYC/AML verification (Onfido) ✅
   - All trading decisions remain user responsibility ✅

3. REQUIRED APPROVALS

   - Risk limits (2% position, 5x leverage, 20% exposure) non-negotiable
   - KYC approval required before live trading
```

```markdown
☑️ FINANCIAL PROMOTION
   AlphaForge is NOT a financial advisor. Signals are generated by AI
   and do NOT constitute investment advice. You alone are responsible
   for all trading decisions.

☑️ PAST PERFORMANCE
   Historical backtest results are NOT indicative of future performance.
   Paper trading performance may not reflect live market conditions.

☑️ LEVERAGE RISK
   Trading with leverage (up to 5x) amplifies losses. You may lose more
   than your initial deposit. Liquidation can occur with 20% margin buffer.

☑️ EXECUTION RISK
   Exchange outages, network delays, or slippage may cause unfavorable
   execution prices. Stop loss orders may not fill at target prices.

☑️ MODEL RISK
   ML models make prediction errors. Signals may be wrong despite high
   confidence scores. Market regimes can change unexpectedly.

☑️ TECHNICAL RISK
   System failures, data corruption, or API errors could prevent trade
   execution or cause unintended trades. AlphaForge is not liable for
   technical failures beyond our control.

☑️ AGE VERIFICATION
   I confirm I am 18 years or older and meet all regulatory requirements
   in my jurisdiction. By using this platform, I accept all risks.
```

### 3.2 Limitation of Liability

```
TO THE MAXIMUM EXTENT PERMITTED BY LAW:

AlphaForge shall not be liable for:
- Trading losses resulting from signal execution
- Market slippage or adverse price movement
- Exchange outages or connectivity issues
- Third-party API failures (Binance, Bybit, etc.)
- Data quality issues or incorrect market data

MAXIMUM LIABILITY: User's subscription fee for that billing cycle.

EXCEPTIONS (Willful Misconduct):
- Unauthorized account access covered by insurance
```

## 5. AML/CFT Compliance (Anti-Money Laundering)

### Risk Assessment
```
LOW RISK:
- Individual retail traders
- Standard Know-Your-Customer (KYC)
- Standard monitoring

MEDIUM RISK:
- High-value accounts (>$100k/month)
- Cryptocurrency source funds
- Frequent large trades

HIGH RISK:
- OFAC/PEP list matches → BLOCK + SAR file
- Shell company indicators → REJECT
- Sanctions country match → BLOCK
```

### SAR (Suspicious Activity Report) Filing

**Triggers for SAR:**
- OFAC/PEP list match (auto-filed within 10 days)
- Structuring pattern detected (multiple small deposits)
- Trading unusual patterns (massive sudden volume)
- Jurisdiction sanctions evasion attempt

**Filing Process:**
1. System automatically flags suspicious pattern
2. Compliance officer reviews within 24 hours
3. SAR filed with FCA/FinCEN within 10 days
4. User account frozen pending investigation
5. Investigation resolution logged in audit trail

## 6. Data Security & Breach Notification

### Encryption Standards
```
DATA AT REST:
- PII: AES-256 encryption (AWS KMS keys)
- Passwords: bcrypt with salt (not stored in plaintext)
- API keys: Encrypted, masked in logs

DATA IN TRANSIT:
- TLS 1.3 minimum for all connections
- Certificate pinning for mobile (Phase 2)
- Signed/encrypted audit trail messages

KEY MANAGEMENT:
- Keys rotated quarterly
- Master keys in HSM (Hardware Security Module)
- Access logged and audited
```

### Breach Notification Timeline
```
DISCOVERY → 24h INVESTIGATION → 72h NOTIFICATION

If 1+ users affected:
- Email notification to affected users
- Notification to FCA/regulators
- Press statement for >1000 users
- Free credit monitoring offered (Phase 2)

Audit Trail Entry:
- Hash-chained breach report in immutable log
- Cryptographic proof of notification timing
```
